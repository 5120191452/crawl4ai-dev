Turn the display off and verify the resulting Android power state.

Parameters:

**timeout_s** – Maximum verification time in seconds.

Returns:

`(error_code, MobileOperationResult)`. Success means the screen-off state was observed; failure is expressed only by `error_code`.

turn_screen_on(_timeout_s =30.0_, _unlock =True_)


Brief:

Wake the display and optionally dismiss a non-secure keyguard.

Parameters:

  * **timeout_s** – Maximum wake and verification time in seconds.
  * **unlock** – When `True` (default), require both an awake display and dismissed Keyguard; when `False`, verify only wake state.


Returns:

`(error_code, MobileOperationResult)`. Success means the requested screen/unlock state was observed. PIN, password and pattern locks are never bypassed and return a state error.

update_file_field(_path_ , _field_ , _value_ , _encoding ='utf-8'_)


Brief:

Replace the specified field content in a text file.

Parameters:

  * **path** – Existing text-file path.
  * **field** – Existing field text used to locate the content to modify.
  * **value** – Replacement text saved in place of `field`.
  * **encoding** – One of `utf-8`, `utf-16`, `utf-16le` or `utf-16be`.


Returns:

`(error_code, MobilePathInfo)`; a missing field returns a not-found error.
